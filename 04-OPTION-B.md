Direction for every plan, elevation and rendering: **front = tongue**, **rear = double doors**, **curb = service-window side**, and **street = opposite/staff side**.

## Approved differences

- Place the entire 90-inch equipment line directly beneath the wide service window.
- Equipment sequence: 12-inch hand sink + 36-inch two-compartment sink/drainboard + 24-inch cocktail station + 18-inch refrigerator.
- The 36-inch two-compartment sink is the locked concept basis. Do not procure it or release the counter until the health authority accepts the two-compartment procedure; otherwise redesign the 90-inch line.
- Optimize reach, sequencing and storage for one bartender.
- Place POS/order at the forward end, cocktail production centrally and garnish/pickup at the rear end.
- Do not provide Option A’s outboard cabinet.
- Provide a shallow illuminated functional back-bar on the opposite wall.

## Locked backwall program

The backwall is a calm, continuous and deliberately asymmetrical **Linear Gallery**. Its composition follows the single bartender's forward/order-to-rear/pickup workflow rather than mirroring the wall. It includes:

- One restrained softly illuminated bottle-display element
- Enclosed positively retained clean-glass storage
- Shallow storage/rails for lightweight service items
- One continuous horizontal warm-light datum coordinated with the copper-painted ceiling and customer-facing glow
- Positive operating and travel restraint for every item

### Concept composition

- Align the primary working-bottle display with the central cocktail-production zone for one-turn access from the 24-inch cocktail station.
- Place enclosed clean-glass storage adjacent to the production/pickup side so the bartender can load, finish and hand off without backtracking.
- Place the shallow lightweight service/tool rail toward the order/production side; it is not a second work counter.
- Keep the visual composition low, long and uninterrupted wherever the verified wall and any retained door permit.
- Preserve one clear visual hierarchy: continuous light line, bottle display, then enclosed glass storage. Do not duplicate the display into matched halves.

The backwall may not include a floor cabinet, full-depth counter, sink, refrigerator or heavy storage. Its exact depth, mounting height, width and attachment remain open until the trailer wall, structure and door locations are measured. It must fit within the verified wall/upper-service envelope and preserve the 32-inch clear aisle, employee movement, equipment access and rear egress. If the acquired trailer retains a side escape door on this wall, the gallery shall stop, bridge only above the verified clear opening where structurally permitted, or break into modules; it shall never cover, narrow or obstruct the door.

## Operating intent

- Primary single-bartender solution.
- Optimized for 75–100 guests and approximately 200 beverages, while retaining the shared 250-beverage design storage/staging basis.
- The backwall improves immediate glass, bottle and lightweight-tool access without creating a second work counter.

## Systems placement

- Place the water heater, pump, strainer and filter in a leak-managed service bay below the 36-inch sink/drainboard module in the under-window line.
- Use the enclosed base at the 12-inch hand-sink end for the dedicated liquid-tight chemical compartment, separated from plumbing and clean storage.
- Use the remaining verified volume below the drainboard for lidded trash/recycling pull-outs and a small rigid broken-glass container; exact catalog bins remain VERIFY.
- Route sink, ice-well and refrigeration drainage through the floor to the centered underfloor waste system; all exterior service remains on the street side.
- Place the dry electrical distribution panel high on the operational face of the front partition; do not place it in the Linear Gallery or wet line.
- Place the extinguisher at the retained street-side staff door and the emergency light above the rear double doors.
- Break, stop or bridge the shallow Linear Gallery around the verified staff-door opening without reducing door clearance or egress.
- Secure removable transit cases low over the axle zone during travel and remove them from the aisle before operation.

## Travel state

- The full under-window line and backwall structure remain installed.
- Backwall display/service surfaces, open rails and counters are empty.
- Bottles, working glasses, tools, blender and POS travel in labeled restrained docks or support-vehicle cases.
