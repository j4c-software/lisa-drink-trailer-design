## Production-method boundary

- Photoreal or atmospheric image generation is reserved for exterior perspectives, customer-experience views, interior perspectives and operating/travel details.
- `PL-01` plans and `EL-01` through `EL-04` elevations are precision graphics derived from the canonical dimensions and equipment schedule. Do not generate them as photographic or AI-improvised images.
- Underfloor tanks, concealed utilities and structural zones appear only in precise plans, sections or presentation overlays—not as visible objects through opaque surfaces.
- Add labels, dimensions, option names, **VERIFY** notes and conditional statements during presentation composition. Do not rely on image generation to spell technical labels correctly.

## Rendering prompt assembly rule

Every future image request shall be assembled in this order:

1. Common trailer/scene brief
2. Applicable exterior or interior material brief
3. Applicable Option A, B or C overlay
4. Applicable view/camera brief
5. Operating or travel-state brief
6. Common prohibitions
7. Image-specific compliance checklist

Do not shorten the assembled brief by removing geometry, equipment, door, material, travel or conditional controls.

## Renderer authority boundary

The renderer may visualize the approved design. It may not:

- Invent, resize, substitute or omit equipment
- Move equipment, sinks, POS, refrigeration, exits or storage
- Change the 90-inch equipment arithmetic
- Change trailer geometry, proportions, roof form, doors, fenders or tongue
- Add, remove, enlarge or relocate openings
- Enlarge the service window beyond the approved maximum
- Reduce the locked aisle or conceal an egress conflict
- Alter the approved material, color or lighting language
- Add custom equipment to solve a dimensional conflict
- Convert a conditional item into an apparently approved solution
- Add decorative objects that interfere with sanitation, travel restraint or workflow
- Copy a third-party listing photograph as final presentation artwork
- Treat a reference image as dimensional or structural evidence

If an approved design cannot be rendered without violating a control, stop and report the conflict. Do not solve it visually.

## Travel versus operating state

- State clearly whether a view shows travel or operation.
- Operating views may show deployed ledge, menu, POS, restrained display and active service kits.
- Travel views must show closed/latching hatch and ledge, clear counters/open shelves, secured doors/drawers and removed or docked bottles, glasses, blender, tools, POS and décor.
- Do not imply that loaded speed rails, loose glassware or counter appliances remain in place during towing.
