## Smallest fixed-shell Option A response

Preserve the defining Option A relationship—a largely open driver/customer window wall and the primary wet-production line on the passenger/utility wall—but redistribute the personal-use equipment:

1. Retain D1 at 30 inches nominal and preserve its outward swing and inside approach.
2. Use a **60-inch passenger service line** at `X = 54–114`: 36-inch two-compartment sink/drainboard, 6-inch glass-rinser/drip reserve and 18-inch Refrigerator 2.
3. Retain 18-inch Refrigerator 1 on the **front-wall return**, provisionally at the driver-side end. Its 24-inch depth occupies the former wish-list front nook, not the main 90-inch longitudinal work zone.
4. Replace the former fixed cocktail/ice station with a removable insulated cocktail-ice bin above Refrigerator 2. Provide contained drainage or a removable catch vessel.
5. Remove the dedicated 12-inch hand sink from the fixed base equipment schedule. For personal use, treat a separate compact handwashing provision as optional pending the measured D1 position, desired operating practice and final plumbing study. Do not imply commercial sanitation approval.
6. Preserve the 32-inch nominal main aisle. At the provisional front refrigerator return, the 18-inch appliance width leaves 38 inches across the 56-inch clear interior before door, trim and service-clearance checks.
