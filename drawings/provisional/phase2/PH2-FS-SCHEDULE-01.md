## Post-acquisition replacement checklist

- [ ] Establish one physical exterior fabricator datum and measured finished-floor datum.
- [ ] Strip/map exterior and interior skin, posts, rails, seams, roof bows and rear portal.
- [ ] Measure wall curvature and complete wall/floor/roof assembly sections.
- [ ] Replace service-opening X/Z assumptions and corner radii.
- [ ] Replace header, jamb, sill and connection reserves with approved structural details.
- [ ] Select hatch, hinges, supports, restraint, latches and confirm reaction loads.
- [ ] Resolve ledge attachment, load, drainage, flashing, seals and travel state.
- [ ] Measure D1 and both rear leaves, including clear openings, swings and hardware.
- [ ] Locate eligible utility and ventilation penetrations after routing coordination.
- [ ] Map frame, crossmembers, wheel wells and suspension travel before floor/tank work.
- [ ] Produce the final cut-point table and 1:1 CAD/vector file only after Gate 5 release.
