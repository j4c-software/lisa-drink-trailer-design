## Approved differences

- Place the entire 90-inch equipment line directly beneath the wide service window.
- Equipment sequence: 12-inch hand sink + 18-inch Station A + 24-inch centered two-compartment sink + 18-inch Station B + 18-inch refrigerator.
- Use two standard Regency 600IB1812 12-inch cocktail stations, each centered within its 18-inch module; the remaining six inches in each module is work/tool space.
- Use the standard Krowne KR19-22C 24-inch compact two-compartment sink as the concept basis.
- Center the shared sink between the two bartender stations and provide compliant splash separation on both sides.
- Match the two stations in tools, bottle allocation, lighting and removable service kits.
- Provide a symmetrical shallow illuminated **Twin-Wing Backbar** on the opposite wall, composed specifically for two equal bartender stations and visually distinct from Option B's continuous Linear Gallery.
- Do not provide an outboard cabinet.

## Locked backwall program

The backwall is a crisp, centered and symmetrical **Twin-Wing Backbar**. It expresses two equal bartender work zones around one shared center. It includes:

- One central restrained softly illuminated reserve-bottle display aligned with the shared sink datum; frequently used working bottles remain at Stations A and B
- Two matched outer enclosed clean-glass storage wings, one aligned to each bartender station
- Two separate shallow service/tool rails for lightweight items, one assigned to Station A and one to Station B
- A three-part lighting rhythm—lit wing, brighter central feature, lit wing—coordinated with the copper-painted ceiling and customer-facing glow
- Balanced placement so each bartender reaches the assigned wing and rail without entering the other bartender's station
- Positive operating and travel restraint for every item

### Concept composition

- Keep the centerline visually aligned with the centered two-compartment sink and use the central display for reserve/show bottles rather than rapid-reach production bottles.
- Mirror the left and right storage wings in visible size, lighting and hardware so the two stations read as equal.
- Keep the station rails physically separate; do not create one continuous shelf that encourages crossover.
- Use a stronger vertical center and shorter horizontal wings so C reads as a two-station cockpit rather than B's long horizontal gallery.
- Maintain one bottle-display feature overall; the two wings are enclosed glass storage, not additional bottle displays.

The backwall may not include a floor cabinet, full-depth counter, sink, refrigerator or heavy storage. Its exact depth, mounting height, width and attachment remain open until the trailer wall, structure and door locations are measured. It must fit within the verified wall/upper-service envelope and preserve the 32-inch clear aisle, two-bartender movement, equipment access and rear egress. Required safety and electrical elements shall remain flush and accessible. If the acquired trailer retains a side escape door on this wall, the composition shall be adapted symmetrically around the verified clear opening where possible; it shall never cover, narrow or obstruct the door.

## Operating intent

- Primary two-bartender solution.
- Optimized for 100–125 guests and up to 250 beverages.
- A 150-guest event remains conditional on a simplified batched menu, exterior runner and expanded protected staging.

## Systems placement

- Place the water heater, pump, strainer and filter in a compact leak-managed service bay beneath the centered 24-inch sink. Final clearance around traps, supply piping and service access remains VERIFY.
- Use the enclosed base at the 12-inch hand-sink end for the dedicated liquid-tight chemical compartment, separated from plumbing and clean storage.
- Divide verified residual space below the centered sink into lidded trash/recycling pull-outs and a small rigid broken-glass container, physically separated from the plumbing bay. The compact fit and exact catalog bins remain VERIFY and may require a controlled layout revision.
- Route both cocktail-station drains, the shared sink, hand sink and refrigeration drainage through the floor to the centered underfloor waste system; no customer-side discharge or exposed hose.
- Place the dry electrical distribution panel high on the operational face of the front partition; do not place it in either Twin-Wing storage zone or a wet area.
- Place the extinguisher at the retained street-side staff door and the emergency light above the rear double doors.
- Adapt the Twin-Wing composition around the verified staff-door opening while retaining balanced station access and full egress.
- Secure the two matched removable station kits in low transit cases over the axle zone during travel and remove them from the aisle before operation.

## Travel state

- Both stations, the shared sink and the backwall display/service surfaces remain empty of loose items.
- Two matched bottle/tool kits and covered glass racks travel in labeled low docks or support-vehicle cases.
- Nothing travels on the centered sink or open service surfaces.
