# Lisa Drink Trailer — Render Brief Matrix

**Revision:** 1.5 — September 25, 2026
**Status:** OPTION A-ONLY IMAGE CONTROL — EQUIPMENT PLACEMENT HOLD

This matrix preserves the approved Option A shell concept for every photographic rendering. It does not replace the Master, Option A, Equipment Inventory, Presentation or Rendering-Control files. Options B and C are superseded historical references and receive no new production. No candidate may show installed equipment until the inventory is explicitly approved and placement restarts from an empty fixed-shell model.

## Universal assembly rule

For every candidate, combine in this order:

1. Presentation-page purpose
2. Common trailer or interior scene brief
3. Option A overlay
4. Approved Option A plan and elevations
5. Camera/view definition
6. Operating or travel state
7. Required people, equipment, setting and labels
8. Common prohibitions and QA checklist

Any conflict stops production. The renderer may not solve a conflict by inventing, moving, substituting or omitting elements.

### Fixed interior orientation

- `IN-01` looks rear-to-front: driver/customer/service wall image-right; passenger/utility/opposite wall image-left.
- `IN-02` looks front-to-rear: driver/customer/service wall image-left; passenger/utility/opposite wall image-right.
- Option A equipment sequence is UNSET. Do not produce `IN-01`, `IN-02`, plan or elevation assets with installed equipment until the inventory is explicitly approved and placement is restarted from scratch.
- Never mirror an interior image.
